Storage and onward movement planned around your cargo and your delivery schedule.

About this service

Goods often need to wait somewhere between arriving at the port and reaching customers. Tell us about your storage duration, cargo type, volume and distribution needs so suitable warehousing and delivery arrangements can be explored.

Because storage is usually one step in a longer journey, we look at it together with your freight and clearance plans, so cargo can move on without avoidable gaps.

What it covers

  • Short-term and longer-term storage options
  • Planning for how goods are received and held
  • Onward distribution to your customers or sites
  • Coordination with your freight and clearance plans

What to tell us to get a quote

  • Type of cargo and how it is packed
  • Volume, and the number of pallets, packages or containers
  • How long you expect to store it
  • Where it needs to go afterwards, and when
  • Any special handling or storage conditions

How it works

  1. Tell us about your shipmentSend the cargo details through the quote form, WhatsApp, phone or email.
  2. We confirm the planWe reply with a proposed plan and a quote.
  3. We coordinate the workBookings, documents and handling are coordinated with authorised carriers and service providers.
  4. You follow progressUse your tracking number on our website to see the status and updates for your shipment.
